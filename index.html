◉ How We Connect

Bonding That Actually Works

Every part of the day is set up so you naturally meet people and click with them. No awkward forced stuff — just reps doing fun things together until friendships form on their own.

01
Squad Mix & Mingle
You'll be put in a color squad with reps from different faculties and levels — mixed on purpose so you meet people outside your usual circle. That's where the magic starts.
02
Rep Circle Sessions
Quick open-floor moments where reps share stories, wins, and what they're working toward. Low pressure, but you'll walk away feeling like you've known these people a long time.
03
Squad Challenges
Team games that make you laugh, compete, and cheer each other on. Nothing brings people together faster than winning — or losing — as a group.
04
The Appreciation Wall
Drop a note for someone you rate in the 250 Reps circle. Say what you've never said out loud. Watch how fast good energy spreads through the room.
05
Closing Circle
We wrap up together — every rep, one big circle. Winners get crowned. Shoutouts go up. You leave knowing exactly who your people are. Don't be surprised if you tear up a little.
